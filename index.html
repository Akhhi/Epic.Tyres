Warranty Support

Lifetime Replacement Warranty

Many eligible used tyres sold by Epic Tyres are covered by our Lifetime Replacement Warranty.

What it can cover: qualifying manufacturing defects and cases where an eligible tyre does not last for the period or performance that Epic Tyres promised for that tyre.

Eligibility: warranty coverage is not automatically included with every tyre. The applicable warranty, promised period and conditions are explained at the time of purchase.

Inspection: if a warranty claim is made, Epic Tyres will inspect the tyre before deciding whether the issue falls within the applicable warranty.

Not every damage is covered: road hazards, punctures, impact damage, misuse, incorrect inflation, fitting-related damage and other causes outside the promised warranty may not qualify.

Please keep your purchase details and contact us if you believe an eligible tyre has developed a covered problem.
